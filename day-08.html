Lava · local dishes of the day

Roula Villa recommends Lava near the end of the Perissa–Perivolos coastal road toward Vlychada: no fixed menu, just dishes of the day. Their tip is to ask for half portions of several dishes. This sounds like a good optional local lunch; ask what suits you and confirm opening and prices before going.

Pyrgos or Emporio · choose one village

For a short traditional-village outing, choose Emporio’s castle lanes and To Kafenedaki tou Emboriou, or Pyrgos with coffee at Kantouni or a view from Franco’s. These are hotel recommendations; hours and prices are unverified. Ask reception where to enter Emporio’s old quarter, which is easy to miss.

Use this instead of another outing on arrival day. Keep 25 Sep close to the hotel until wedding timing is confirmed, and 26 Sep free for checkout, luggage and the 15:45 ferry.

24 SEP · SANTORINI

To Psaraki · Vlychada harbour

Regional or food illustration; not a photograph of this venue.

A seafood lunch or early dinner option after settling into Perissa, if you feel like an outing. Suggested order from the published menu: Santorini fava, grilled sardines and boiled greens; add shrimp saganaki for tomato, feta and ouzo flavours.

This needs a taxi or arranged transport from Perissa. Confirm a table and the return ride. For whole fish, ask the weight and total price before ordering.

Fava, capers & tomato fritters

Try the local golden pulse purée with olive oil, onion and capers. Tomatokeftedes are tomato fritters: worth tasting, but usually made with flour. Ask how each dish is prepared.

Roula Villa · getting your bearings

Hotel tips shared 19 Sep: the shortcut beside the entrance leads to Perissa’s Christ Church square, beach and nearest bus stop in about five minutes. Ask reception to point it out; the main road has a mini-market and a bakery the hotel describes as open 24 hours.

For Oia, change buses in Fira. The hotel’s frequency and express travel estimates are not a checked timetable and exclude waiting and traffic. Ask for the current last return. Perissa–Kamari water taxis depend on operation and sea conditions; confirm the final boat back with the captain.
