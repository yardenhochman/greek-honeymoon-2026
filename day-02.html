Food & experiences

18 SEP · RETHYMNO STOP

1600 Raki BaRaki · a proper first lunch

Make the first full day a food occasion: stop in Rethymno on the drive to Chania for a leisurely Cretan lunch at Raki BaRaki, part of Avli. The restaurant specifically welcomes lunch and serves shared dishes with creative local cooking.

Plan around 12:30–13:00, allowing for the 10:00 rental pickup, driving, parking and a short walk into the old town. Leave roughly two hours for lunch and a wander, then continue to Chania for an afternoon check-in. These times are planning estimates; confirm the table and kitchen hours.

Ask about vegetable dishes, mushrooms, cheese meze and the day’s seafood options. Avli’s courtyard restaurant is a more elaborate alternative if it can offer a suitable lunch sitting.

Lake Kournas · an optional pause after lunch

After Rethymno, this is a pleasant detour before Chania: a lakeside walk, a drink with a view or a pedal boat if available. Choose this as the day’s extra stop; allow roughly 60–90 minutes at the lake, plus the detour.

Booking: no reservation for a simple lakeside visit. Boat availability and operating conditions must be checked locally. Bring water, a hat and comfortable shoes; add swimwear and a towel if planning to swim.

Adding this stop moves the Chania arrival later than the basic 15:30–16:30 plan. Skip it if lunch runs long or you prefer more time in Chania.

Melidoni cave · an alternative before Rethymno

Regional or food illustration; not a photograph of this venue.

Choose the cave instead of Lake Kournas if a short underground visit appeals more. It lies inland before Rethymno when driving from Heraklion; allow around 45–60 minutes for the visit, plus driving and parking. These are planning estimates.

Booking: check current opening hours, ticket arrangements and access with the operator before going; advance-booking requirements have not been confirmed. Bring shoes with grip and a light layer. A morning visit may require a later lunch reservation.

18–19 SEP · CHANIA

To Maridaki · seafood in Splantzia

Regional or food illustration; not a photograph of this venue.

A good fit for the Chania evening: a changing fish, seafood and vegetable menu close to the old-town stay. Ask what is fresh, then build a shared table around fish and seasonal vegetables.

Official site says closed Sundays: choose Friday or Saturday, not departure day. Call to confirm a table. Daskalogianni 33.

Prepare & pack

Before you go

  • Confirm the 10:00 car pickup point. Have the licence, rental documents and payment card required by the rental company ready.
  • Call Raki BaRaki / Avli to check lunch availability. Choose one optional stop and allow time for parking and the walk into Rethymno.

Bring along

  • Comfortable walking shoes, water, hat, sunscreen and sunglasses.
  • If adding a beach stop: swimwear, a compact towel and a bag for wet clothes.
  • Car charger, downloaded route and an accessible day bag; keep luggage out of sight.
