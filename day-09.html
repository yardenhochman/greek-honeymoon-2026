Prepare & pack

Before you go

  • Confirm the wedding’s exact time, venue and transport with the family. Allow time to return, shower and get ready.
  • Arrange tomorrow’s port taxi and ask about keeping luggage after the 11:00 checkout.

Bring along

  • Wedding clothes, suitable shoes and any accessories; a light layer for the evening.
  • For a short morning swim: swimwear, towel and sun protection. Keep dry clothes ready.

Useful today

Hotel ↔ wedding

First confirm the venue, start time and whether the hosts have arranged transport. If needed, ask Roula Villa to arrange a taxi there and agree the return plan. No wedding transfer is booked.

Roula Villa · getting your bearings

Hotel tips shared 19 Sep: the shortcut beside the entrance leads to Perissa’s Christ Church square, beach and nearest bus stop in about five minutes. Ask reception to point it out; the main road has a mini-market and a bakery the hotel describes as open 24 hours.

For Oia, change buses in Fira. The hotel’s frequency and express travel estimates are not a checked timetable and exclude waiting and traffic. Ask for the current last return. Perissa–Kamari water taxis depend on operation and sea conditions; confirm the final boat back with the captain.
