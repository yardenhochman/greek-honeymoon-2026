Loutro or Glyka Nera · keep the boat flexible

A village wander in Loutro or a longer swim at Glyka Nera can be today’s extra experience. Choose according to the confirmed boats and weather, not both at any cost. Arrange the necessary boat seats locally and confirm the last return; bring beach gear and a waterproof pouch.

SFAKIA

Sfakian pita

A thin cheese-filled pie with honey: a regional treat to ask for around Sfakia or Loutro. It contains wheat; consider sharing one for the taste.

A quiet evening in Plakias

Regional or food illustration; not a photograph of this venue.

After the coastal walk or boat, leave time to shower and choose dinner near the hotel. Ask Sofia for an open local taverna; look for grilled fish, greens and vegetable dishes. No particular restaurant has been verified here.

Prepare & pack

Before you go

  • Confirm the Loutro / Glyka Nera boat schedule and the last return before committing to a beach stop. Keep the Sfakia road alternative available.
  • Check current access before adding Kourtaliotiko. Ask Sofia about laundry timing before dropping off clothes.

Bring along

  • Swimwear, towel, sun protection, drinking water and snacks for the beach.
  • Shoes with grip for rocky paths, dry clothes and a waterproof pouch for phone and documents.
  • Laundry in a separate bag if the hotel confirms a suitable service.
