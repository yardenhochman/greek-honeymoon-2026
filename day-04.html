A slow dinner at Akrogiali

Regional or food illustration; not a photograph of this venue.

You are already sleeping above the taverna in Lykos. Make that the experience: swim, shower, sit down with a view and ask what the kitchen has that day.

Look for grilled fish, greens with lemon and olive oil, then a Sfakian cheese-and-honey pie if available. This is our low-effort route pick, not a verified daily menu.

Sfakian pita

Prepare & pack

Before you go

  • Confirm the selected gorge, weather and current access before setting off; choose the easier alternative if conditions do not suit.
  • Agree parking, luggage and the last leg to Akrogiali with the property. Confirm boat timing and the return from the walk; do not assume an on-demand boat.

Bring along

  • Walking shoes with grip, enough water for the full walk, snacks, hat and sunscreen.
  • Small day bag, charged phone, offline route, compact first-aid kit and a light overnight bag if luggage stays with the car.
  • Swimwear, towel and a dry bag if adding a swim or boat ride.

Packing checklist →
