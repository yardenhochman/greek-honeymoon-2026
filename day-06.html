Food & experiences

22 SEP · SOUTH COAST

Choose one beach to linger at

After Preveli, choose a longer pause at Triopetra or Agios Pavlos instead of treating both as compulsory stops. Bring a picnic or ask your hotel for an open seasonal taverna.

Leave enough daylight for the drive to Agia Galini. A relaxed swim and lunch can be the signature experience of this day.

See this day’s route →

No table reservation needed for a self-organised picnic. Bring water, food, swimwear and a towel; walking shoes for Preveli.

Prepare & pack

Before you go

  • Choose which beaches to keep; allow energy and time for the climb back from Preveli. Check wind and sea conditions before driving to exposed beaches.
  • Ask Astoria whether laundry can be returned dry before tomorrow’s checkout.

Bring along

  • Swimwear, compact towel, water, snacks, hat and sunscreen.
  • Walking shoes for the steps and paths, beach footwear and a bag for wet swimwear.

Packing checklist →

Beach towels are confirmed at Sofia and Astoria. Bring a compact towel for other stays where they are not confirmed.
