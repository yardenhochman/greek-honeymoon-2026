Harbour, backstreets & a long lunch

Walk the Venetian harbour and Splantzia at an easy pace. Share dakos, seasonal vegetables and fish. On 19 Sep, choose between a full city day and the optional Falasarna outing; do not squeeze both into a checklist.

18–19 SEP · CHANIA

To Maridaki · seafood in Splantzia

Regional or food illustration; not a photograph of this venue.

A good fit for the Chania evening: a changing fish, seafood and vegetable menu close to the old-town stay. Ask what is fresh, then build a shared table around fish and seasonal vegetables.

Official site says closed Sundays: choose Friday or Saturday, not departure day. Call to confirm a table. Daskalogianni 33.

Prepare & pack

Before you go

  • Choose a relaxed Chania day or Falasarna; leave time for the return drive if staying for sunset.
  • For a restaurant you particularly want, ask for a table ahead of time. Ordinary walks need no reservation.
  • Ask Polixeny’s reception for the free beach towels before leaving for Falasarna; no deposit is required.

Bring along

  • City walk: comfortable shoes, water and sun protection.
  • Beach option: swimwear, towel, water shoes if useful, dry clothes and a light layer for the evening.

Packing checklist →

Beach towels are confirmed at Polixeny’s, Sofia and Astoria. Bring a compact towel for other stays where they are not confirmed.
