Food & experiences

Falasarna · today’s flexible schedule

Aim to leave at 10:00–10:30 and arrive around 11:00–11:45. Navigate to Playa Paraiso, find shade, swim and have a light lunch around 13:30–14:30. Return whenever you feel ready; sunset is not a commitment.

Playa Paraiso · start here

Our navigation target on the main organised beach. Umbrellas, sunbeds, coffee, juices, fruit salads and snacks are advertised. Settle here if the shade and price suit you.

Nothing reserved. Ask about umbrella availability, the price for two sunbeds and any minimum spend before settling in.

Kyma Fotis · easy beach lunch

A beach-bar alternative with sandwiches, salads, coffee and beach service. The local tourism listing also lists showers, toilets, changing rooms and parking.

Online menu checked 19 Sep: sandwiches €4–5.50, Greek salad €8, dakos €6 and freddo cappuccino €4. Allow roughly €8–15 per person for light food and a soft drink or coffee, excluding sunbeds. Confirm prices onsite.

Prepare & pack

Before you go

  • Check wind and waves before leaving; aim for 10:00–10:30 if ready, with 1–1h15 driving. Later departure simply shifts the beach time.
  • Umbrella and sunbeds are not booked; check availability and price on arrival. Keep lunch and the return time flexible.
  • Ask Polixeny’s reception for the free beach towels before leaving for Falasarna; no deposit is required.
  • Keep laundry in a separate bag; ask reception about the laundrette’s hours and drying options before heading out.

Bring along

  • Swimwear, hotel beach towels, hats, sunglasses, sunscreen and plenty of water.
  • Sandals, dry clothes, a wet bag, phone and car charger.
