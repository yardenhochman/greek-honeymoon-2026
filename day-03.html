Food & experiences

Harbour, backstreets & a long lunch

Walk the Venetian harbour and Splantzia at an easy pace. Share dakos, seasonal vegetables and fish. On 19 Sep, choose between a full city day and the optional Falasarna outing; do not squeeze both into a checklist.

18–19 SEP · CHANIA

To Maridaki · seafood in Splantzia

A good fit for the Chania evening: a changing fish, seafood and vegetable menu close to the old-town stay. Ask what is fresh, then build a shared table around fish and seasonal vegetables.

Official site says closed Sundays: choose Friday or Saturday, not departure day. Call to confirm a table. Daskalogianni 33.
