Before closing the bags

  • Suggested target: no more than 7.5 kg per trolley and 3.5 kg per backpack. These are packing targets, not measured weights. If an empty trolley weighs 3 kg, aim for about 4.5 kg of contents.
  • Use each backpack as the day bag during the trip. A full-size hiking backpack is not automatically a compliant personal item: measure it when packed.
  • At airport security on the return from Greece, keep ordinary liquids in containers of up to 100 ml, all fitting in one resealable transparent 1-litre bag per person. Put shared liquids into one of those two bags, not a third one.
  • Keep passports, valuables, essential medication and the power bank in the small bags. If a trolley is taken into the hold at the gate, remove batteries and electronics that need to remain in the cabin. Check your power bank against the airline’s battery rules.
  • Pack the first night’s sleepwear and wash kit near the top. Keep wedding outfits together and away from liquids; leave room rather than compressing them under shoes.
  • Before the Lykos overnight, ask Akrogiali where the car and large bags will stay. Prepare a small overnight pouch with the next day’s clothes, wash kit and beach items; transfer it into the day bags once on the ground.
  • Sofia and Astoria confirmed beach towels; other stays are unconfirmed. None of Sofia, Astoria or Roula offers in-house laundry, but they recommend nearby laundries. Prices and turnaround still need checking, so the packing quantities do not rely on a laundry service.
  • If space is tight: remove optional water shoes, duplicate chargers, extra footwear and full-size toiletries first. A laptop, hairdryer and bulky beach blanket are not part of the base list.
  • Adi’s flip-flops, sandals and Shoresh sandals are listed separately so nothing is forgotten. If regular sandals and Shoresh are the same pair, pack it once. If weight is tight, choose which separate pairs you actually need.
