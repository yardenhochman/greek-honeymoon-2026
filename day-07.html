Prepare & pack

Before you go

  • Pick Phaistos or Matala as the main optional stop. Check opening and last-entry times for the archaeological site before leaving.
  • Confirm the rental return bay and fuel policy. Aim to reach the return area by 18:00 for the 19:00 deadline.
  • Prepare tomorrow’s ferry tickets and a small morning bag tonight; confirm the early ride to the port.

Bring along

  • Sun protection, water and comfortable shoes; swimwear and towel only if keeping Matala beach.
  • Rental paperwork, fuel receipt if relevant, and all loose belongings from the car.
