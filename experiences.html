A little menu decoder

A taste of Greek cooking; this photograph is not from one of the recommended restaurants.

CRETAN TABLE

Dakos & horta

Dakos is a crunchy barley-rusk base with tomato, cheese and olive oil. Horta are cooked greens, usually served with lemon and oil. A tomato salad without the rusk is an easy alternative when you prefer to skip bread.

VEGETABLES TO SHARE

Gemista & briam

Look for stuffed tomatoes or peppers (gemista), and oven-roasted vegetables (briam). Ask whether the gemista filling includes meat. Pair with grilled fish or a simple bean dish for a generous shared lunch.

SFAKIA

Sfakian pita

A thin cheese-filled pie with honey: a regional treat to ask for around Sfakia or Loutro. It contains wheat; consider sharing one for the taste.

SANTORINI

Fava, capers & tomato fritters

Try the local golden pulse purée with olive oil, onion and capers. Tomatokeftedes are tomato fritters: worth tasting, but usually made with flour. Ask how each dish is prepared.

AT THE TABLE

A useful ordering shortcut

Try: “Grilled fish, greens, a vegetable dish and fava to share.” Ask whether fish is floured before grilling or frying, and whether sauces contain flour. Menus and kitchen preparation vary; none of these venues is labelled gluten-free here.
