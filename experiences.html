Our field notes · September 2026

Taste, wander, listen.

A few things worth making room for along our route. These are suggestions, not reservations: choose what feels right on the day.

If we only choose three…

A seafood table in Chania. A long, unhurried evening in Lykos. One special Cretan meal in Heraklion. In Santorini, keep the wedding spacious and pick at most one extra outing.

Worth a stop

18–19 SEP · CHANIA

To Maridaki · seafood in Splantzia

A good fit for the Chania evening: a changing fish, seafood and vegetable menu close to the old-town stay. Ask what is fresh, then build a shared table around fish and seasonal vegetables.

Official site says closed Sundays: choose Friday or Saturday, not departure day. Call to confirm a table. Daskalogianni 33.

20–21 SEP · SFAKIA

A slow dinner at Akrogiali

You are already sleeping above the taverna in Lykos. Make that the experience: swim, shower, sit down with a view and ask what the kitchen has that day.

Look for grilled fish, greens with lemon and olive oil, then a Sfakian cheese-and-honey pie if available. This is our low-effort route pick, not a verified daily menu.

22 SEP · SOUTH COAST

Choose one beach to linger at

After Preveli, choose a longer pause at Triopetra or Agios Pavlos instead of treating both as compulsory stops. Bring a picnic or ask your hotel for an open seasonal taverna.

Leave enough daylight for the drive to Agia Galini. A relaxed swim and lunch can be the signature experience of this day.

23 OR 26 SEP · HERAKLION

Peskesi · the special Cretan meal

Our pick for one more deliberate food experience. The restaurant also advertises an olive-oil tasting with food pairing (about two hours, by arrangement).

Choose a meal on 26 Sep after settling in, or a planned daytime slot on 23 Sep that still protects the 19:00 car return. The tasting is optional and unbooked; check availability.

24 SEP · SANTORINI

To Psaraki · Vlychada harbour

A seafood lunch or early dinner option after settling into Perissa, if you feel like an outing. Suggested order from the published menu: Santorini fava, grilled sardines and boiled greens; add shrimp saganaki for tomato, feta and ouzo flavours.

This needs a taxi or arranged transport from Perissa. Confirm a table and the return ride. For whole fish, ask the weight and total price before ordering.

24 SEP · OPTIONAL SWAP

Santo Wines · a caldera tasting

Choose this instead of the Vlychada outing if the view and a wine tasting appeal more. The visitor centre is in Pyrgos; arrange transport from Perissa and book directly.

Keep 25 Sep for the wedding. On 26 Sep, prioritise luggage and the 15:45 ferry rather than adding a winery visit.

A little menu decoder

A taste of Greek cooking; this photograph is not from one of the recommended restaurants.

CRETAN TABLE

Dakos & horta

Dakos is a crunchy barley-rusk base with tomato, cheese and olive oil. Horta are cooked greens, usually served with lemon and oil. A tomato salad without the rusk is an easy alternative when you prefer to skip bread.

VEGETABLES TO SHARE

Gemista & briam

Look for stuffed tomatoes or peppers (gemista), and oven-roasted vegetables (briam). Ask whether the gemista filling includes meat. Pair with grilled fish or a simple bean dish for a generous shared lunch.

SFAKIA

Sfakian pita

A thin cheese-filled pie with honey: a regional treat to ask for around Sfakia or Loutro. It contains wheat; consider sharing one for the taste.

SANTORINI

Fava, capers & tomato fritters

Try the local golden pulse purée with olive oil, onion and capers. Tomatokeftedes are tomato fritters: worth tasting, but usually made with flour. Ask how each dish is prepared.

AT THE TABLE

A useful ordering shortcut

Try: “Grilled fish, greens, a vegetable dish and fava to share.” Ask whether fish is floured before grilling or frying, and whether sauces contain flour. Menus and kitchen preparation vary; none of these venues is labelled gluten-free here.
